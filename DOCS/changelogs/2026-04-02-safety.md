# v3.6.1 — Exit Buffer Equity Fix + Fee Floor Enforcement (2026-04-02)

Three safety bugs found and fixed. All related to positions exiting through the hot-path exit gate.

## Bug 1: Kill Switch False Trigger (Exit Buffer Equity Gap)

**Root cause:** PositionExitGate (hot path) clears the bitmap bit instantly. DrainExits (slow path) credits balance later. Between these events, `equity = balance + Portfolio_ComputeValue` is missing the exited position's value — it's in neither. The kill switch sees a phantom equity crash and fires.

**Previous fix (approximate):** Added `current_price × qty` for pending exits. Wrong because it uses market price instead of actual exit price, and ignores fees and slippage.

**Airtight fix:** New `ExitBuffer_PendingProceeds()` helper in Portfolio.hpp computes exact net proceeds matching what `RecordExit` will credit: `(exit_price - slippage) × qty - fees`. Used in the hot-path kill check. Zero cost on the common case (no pending exits).

**Files:** Portfolio.hpp (helper), PortfolioController.hpp (kill check ~line 654)

## Bug 2: Win/Loss Misclassification

**Root cause:** `RecordExit` counted wins as `reason == 0` (TP exit) regardless of P&L. A TP exit where round-trip fees exceeded gross profit was counted as a "win" — inflating win rate and masking fee floor violations.

**Fix:** Win requires TP exit AND positive P&L. A TP exit that loses money after fees counts as a loss.

**Files:** PortfolioController.hpp (RecordExit ~line 370)

## Bug 3: Fee Floor Violation After Regime Transition

**Root cause:** Fee floor (TP >= entry + entry × fee_rate × 3) was only enforced at fill time. Three regime transitions tighten TP with `FPN_Min` without re-checking:
- TRENDING/MILD_TREND/DOWNTREND → RANGING (line 408)
- TRENDING → MILD_TREND (line 432)
- any → TRENDING_DOWN (line 446)

When volatility drops after fill (common: enter during spike, regime calms), the tightened TP falls below fee breakeven. Position hits TP, exits for gross +$5, pays $6 in fees, net -$1.

**Live example:** Entry $66,749, exit $66,872 (TP), fee $4.27+$1.50 = $5.77, gross +$5.54, net -$1.93. The TP was tightened by a regime transition below the $200 fee floor.

**Fix:** Added fee floor enforcement (`FPN_Max(tp, fee_floor)`) after every `FPN_Min` on TP in `Regime_AdjustPositions`. The fee floor is computed identically to fill time.

**Files:** Strategies/RegimeDetector.hpp (3 locations in Regime_AdjustPositions)
